Review the literature report against the original task and acceptance criteria.
Web sources are valid evidence even when the repository has no bibliography or
notes; repository files are optional context. Support factual claims with
scholarly records and web pages actually inspected, and cite their URLs.
Check relevance, coverage, grouping, and ranking for the requested subject.
Confirm authors, title, year, venue or publisher, and DOI, arXiv ID, or ISBN
against the cited records. Mark entries without a confirming record as
unconfirmed. Check citation counts against their named source and date when
available, and distinguish preprints from peer-reviewed work. Check any reported
retractions or corrections against their sources. Ensure BibTeX matches the
confirmed records and that the report states its search strategy, date, gaps,
and suggested next searches. Do not invent references, identifiers, or evidence.
